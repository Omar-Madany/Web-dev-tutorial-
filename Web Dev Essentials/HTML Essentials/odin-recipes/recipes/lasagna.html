Creamy Chicken Lasagna Recipe

Creamy chicken lasagna is a rich and delicious baked dish made with tender chicken, lasagna noodles, creamy cheese, tomato sauce, and melted mozzarella cheese.

Ingredients

For the Chicken and Lasagna

  • 6 uncooked lasagna noodles
  • 3 skinless, boneless chicken breast halves
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese, divided
  • 1 (8 ounce) package cream cheese, softened
  • ¼ cup hot water
  • 1 cube chicken bouillon
  • 1 (26 ounce) jar spaghetti sauce

For the Bechamel Sauce

  • 5 tablespoons butter
  • 1 cube chicken bouillon
  • 5 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 3 cups milk
  • Ground black pepper to taste

For the Topping

  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese

Instructions

  1. Prepare the oven. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles. Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Cook the lasagna noodles for about 8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until tender but still firm. Drain, rinse with cold water, and set aside.
  3. Cook the chicken. Place the chicken breasts in a large saucepan and add enough water to cover them. Bring to a boil and cook for about 20 minutes, or until the chicken is no longer pink and the juices run clear. Remove the chicken and shred it.
  4. Prepare the chicken mixture. Combine the shredded chicken, 1 cup of mozzarella cheese, and softened cream cheese in a large bowl. Dissolve the chicken bouillon in the hot water. Pour the mixture over the chicken and stir until well combined.
  5. Assemble the lasagna. Spread 1/3 of the spaghetti sauce over the bottom of a 9 × 13-inch baking dish. Add half of the chicken mixture and top with 3 lasagna noodles. Repeat the layers. Finish with the remaining spaghetti sauce and sprinkle the remaining mozzarella cheese on top.
  6. Bake the lasagna. Bake in the preheated oven for about 45 minutes, or until hot and bubbly.
  7. Serve. Allow the lasagna to cool slightly, then serve and enjoy!

Enjoy your homemade creamy chicken lasagna!
