Egyptian Koshary Recipe

Egyptian koshary is a popular and comforting Egyptian dish made with rice, lentils, macaroni, chickpeas, and crispy fried onions. It is traditionally served with a spicy tomato sauce and garlic vinegar sauce.

Ingredients

For the Koshary

  • 1 cup brown lentils
  • 1 cup white rice
  • 1 cup small macaroni
  • 1 cup spaghetti or vermicelli, broken into small pieces
  • 1 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed

For the Tomato Sauce

  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 1 tablespoon vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • ½ teaspoon chili powder
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

For the Garlic Vinegar Sauce

  • ¼ cup vinegar
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • ½ teaspoon ground cumin
  • ½ teaspoon chili powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tablespoons water

For the Fried Onions

  • 2 large onions, thinly sliced
  • ½ cup all-purpose flour
  • Vegetable oil for frying
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Cook the lentils. Rinse the lentils and place them in a pot with enough water. Bring to a boil and cook until the lentils are tender but still firm. Drain and set aside.
  2. Cook the rice. Rinse the rice and cook it with water and a little salt until it is tender and the water has been absorbed. Set aside.
  3. Cook the pasta. Bring a pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the macaroni and broken spaghetti until tender. Drain and set aside.
  4. Prepare the tomato sauce. Heat the vegetable oil in a saucepan. Add the minced garlic and cook for about 30 seconds. Add the tomato sauce, vinegar, cumin, chili powder, salt, and black pepper. Simmer for about 10 to 15 minutes.
  5. Prepare the garlic vinegar sauce. Mix the vinegar, minced garlic, cumin, chili powder, salt, and water in a small bowl. Set aside until serving.
  6. Prepare the fried onions. Toss the sliced onions with flour and a little salt. Heat the vegetable oil in a deep pan and fry the onions until golden brown and crispy. Remove them and place them on a paper towel to drain.
  7. Prepare the chickpeas. Drain and rinse the chickpeas. Heat them gently in a small saucepan or microwave until warmed through.
  8. Assemble the koshary. Place a layer of rice in a serving bowl, followed by the cooked lentils and pasta. Add the chickpeas and top with crispy fried onions.
  9. Serve the koshary. Pour the tomato sauce and garlic vinegar sauce over the koshary according to your taste. Serve hot and enjoy!

Enjoy your homemade Egyptian koshary!
