Bechamel Chicken Recipe

Bechamel chicken pasta is a rich and creamy baked dish made with tender chicken, pasta, vegetables, white bechamel sauce, and melted mozzarella cheese.

Ingredients

For the Chicken and Pasta

  • 1 (16 ounce) package dried penne pasta
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 1 green bell pepper, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 skinless, boneless chicken breasts, cubed
  • 1 bunch cilantro
  • 1 (6 ounce) can Italian-flavored tomato paste
  • ½ cup frozen peas
  • ½ cup water, or more or less as needed
  • 3 sprigs fresh dill, chopped
  • Salt and ground black pepper to taste

For the Bechamel Sauce

  • 5 tablespoons butter
  • 1 cube chicken bouillon
  • 5 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 3 cups milk
  • Ground black pepper to taste

For the Topping

  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta. Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Add the penne pasta and cook for 8 to 10 minutes, or until al dente. Drain and set aside.
  2. Prepare the chicken mixture. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the onion, bell pepper, and garlic. Cook and stir until the onion is tender, about 5 minutes. Add the chicken and cilantro and cook until the chicken is no longer pink in the center.
  3. Finish the chicken mixture. Stir in the tomato paste, peas, water, dill, salt, and pepper. Cook for another 5 minutes.
  4. Prepare the bechamel sauce. Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Stir in the chicken bouillon cube and flour until smooth. Gradually whisk in the milk. Bring the mixture to a boil and cook for about 2 minutes, or until the sauce thickens. Season with black pepper.
  5. Prepare the oven. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Lightly grease a 9 × 13-inch baking dish.
  6. Assemble the dish. Add half of the cooked pasta to the baking dish, followed by half of the chicken mixture, half of the bechamel sauce, and half of the mozzarella cheese. Repeat the layers.
  7. Bake the pasta. Bake in the preheated oven for 30 minutes, or until the dish is bubbling and golden brown on top.

Enjoy your homemade bechamel chicken pasta!
